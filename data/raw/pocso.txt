ACT NAME: PROTECTION OF CHILDREN FROM SEXUAL OFFENCES ACT 2012 (POCSO)
ACT NUMBER: 32 OF 2012
RELEVANCE: Medical, Legal, Child Protection, Sexual Assault, Reporting Obligations

---

SECTION 2: DEFINITIONS
2(d) "child" means any person below the age of eighteen years.
2(f) "penetrative sexual assault" has the same meaning as assigned to it in section 3.
2(g) "prescribed" means prescribed by rules made under this Act.
2(i) "sexual assault" has the same meaning as assigned to it in section 7.
2(j) "sexual harassment" has the same meaning as assigned to it in section 11.
2(l) "Special Court" means a court designated as such under section 28.

---

SECTION 3: PENETRATIVE SEXUAL ASSAULT
A person is said to commit penetrative sexual assault if he penetrates his penis into the vagina, mouth, urethra or anus of a child; or inserts any object or part of body into the vagina, urethra or anus of the child; or manipulates any part of the body of the child so as to cause penetration; or applies his mouth to the penis, vagina, anus, urethra of the child.

---

SECTION 4: PUNISHMENT FOR PENETRATIVE SEXUAL ASSAULT
Whoever commits penetrative sexual assault shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than seven years but which may extend to imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.

---

SECTION 5: AGGRAVATED PENETRATIVE SEXUAL ASSAULT
Aggravated penetrative sexual assault is committed when the offender is a police officer, armed forces member, public servant, hospital staff, educational institution staff, or management of a jail or remand home. Also applies when the assault involves gang assault, deadly weapons, causes grievous hurt, makes the child pregnant, inflicts HIV, involves a child below twelve years, is committed by a relative or person in a position of trust, or is committed repeatedly.

---

SECTION 6: PUNISHMENT FOR AGGRAVATED PENETRATIVE SEXUAL ASSAULT
Whoever commits aggravated penetrative sexual assault shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than ten years but which may extend to imprisonment for life and shall also be liable to fine.

---

SECTION 7: SEXUAL ASSAULT
Whoever, with sexual intent, touches the vagina, penis, anus or breast of the child or makes the child touch such body parts, or does any other act with sexual intent which involves physical contact without penetration, is said to commit sexual assault.

---

SECTION 8: PUNISHMENT FOR SEXUAL ASSAULT
Whoever commits sexual assault shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than three years but which may extend to five years, and shall also be liable to fine.

---

SECTION 9: AGGRAVATED SEXUAL ASSAULT
Aggravated sexual assault is committed when the offender is a police officer, armed forces member, public servant, hospital staff, educational institution staff, or management of a jail or remand home. Also applies when assault involves deadly weapons, causes grievous hurt, inflicts HIV, involves a child below twelve years, is committed by a relative or person in a position of trust, or is committed repeatedly.

---

SECTION 10: PUNISHMENT FOR AGGRAVATED SEXUAL ASSAULT
Whoever commits aggravated sexual assault shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than five years but which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.

---

SECTION 11: SEXUAL HARASSMENT
A person commits sexual harassment upon a child when with sexual intent he utters words or makes gestures intended to be seen or heard by the child; makes a child exhibit his body; shows pornographic material to a child; repeatedly follows or contacts a child; threatens to use any depiction of the child's body in media; or entices a child for pornographic purposes.

---

SECTION 12: PUNISHMENT FOR SEXUAL HARASSMENT
Whoever commits sexual harassment upon a child shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years and shall also be liable to fine.

---

SECTION 19: REPORTING OF OFFENCES — MANDATORY DOCTOR OBLIGATION
Any person INCLUDING A DOCTOR who has apprehension that an offence under this Act is likely to be committed or has knowledge that such an offence has been committed SHALL report such information to the Special Juvenile Police Unit or the local police.
FAILURE TO REPORT IS A CRIMINAL OFFENCE under Section 21.
The report must be recorded in writing and given an entry number.
Where the child needs care and protection, immediate arrangement must be made to admit the child to a shelter home or nearest hospital within twenty-four hours.
The Special Juvenile Police Unit or local police shall report the matter to the Child Welfare Committee within twenty-four hours.
No person shall incur civil or criminal liability for giving information in good faith.

---

SECTION 20: OBLIGATION OF HOSPITAL STAFF TO REPORT
Any personnel of a hospital, irrespective of the number of persons employed, who comes across any material or object which is sexually exploitative of a child SHALL provide such information to the Special Juvenile Police Unit or local police.

---

SECTION 21: PUNISHMENT FOR FAILURE TO REPORT
Any person who fails to report the commission of an offence under Section 19 shall be punished with imprisonment which may extend to six months or with fine or with both.
Any person in-charge of an institution who fails to report shall be punished with imprisonment up to one year and with fine.
THIS PROVISION DOES NOT APPLY TO THE CHILD VICTIM.

---

SECTION 23: PROCEDURE FOR MEDIA — IDENTITY PROTECTION
No person shall disclose the identity of a child including name, address, photograph, family details, school, neighbourhood or any other particulars which may lead to disclosure of identity of the child.
DOCTOR OBLIGATION: Do not share any identifying information about the child victim with media or any unauthorised party.

---

SECTION 27: MEDICAL EXAMINATION OF A CHILD
The medical examination of a child in respect of whom any offence has been committed under this Act SHALL be conducted notwithstanding that a First Information Report or complaint has not been registered.
IMPORTANT: Medical examination does NOT require FIR to be filed first. Treat and examine immediately.
If the victim is a girl child, the medical examination SHALL be conducted by a woman doctor.
The medical examination shall be conducted in the presence of the parent of the child or any other person in whom the child reposes trust or confidence.
If no parent or trusted person is available, examination shall be conducted in the presence of a woman nominated by the head of the medical institution.

---

SECTION 41: MEDICAL TREATMENT EXEMPTION
The provisions of sections 3 to 13 shall NOT apply in case of medical examination or medical treatment of a child when such medical examination or treatment is undertaken with the consent of the child's parents or guardian.
DOCTOR NOTE: Routine medical treatment with parental consent is fully protected under this section.

---

SECTION 42: ALTERNATE PUNISHMENT
Where an act constitutes an offence under this Act and also under IPC sections 354A, 354B, 375, 376, 376A, 376D or section 509, the offender shall be liable to punishment under whichever law provides the greater punishment.
